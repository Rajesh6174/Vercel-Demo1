How It Works

From general ledger to CFO commentary in five steps

Read-only integrations and human-reviewed AI at every step — your finance team stays in control.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Securely connect your ERP, GL, and spreadsheets with read-only access — typically live within a week.

  2. 02

    Consolidate

    AI maps and normalizes your chart of accounts into a single, consistent P&L structure.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    The platform detects variances, trends, and anomalies against budget and prior periods automatically.

  4. 04

    Narrate

    AI drafts CFO-ready commentary explaining the "why" behind every material movement.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    The live P&L and commentary land in your CFO dashboard, Slack, or board deck — reviewed, not re-typed.
